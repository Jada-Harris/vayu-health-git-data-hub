Glossary

Plain-English meanings of the terms and abbreviations used in the Data Dictionary and on Vayu Health dashboards.

TermWhat it means
A1cA blood test that shows a person's average blood sugar over about the last three months. Also written HbA1c. Higher numbers mean less controlled diabetes.
Active during monthA member whose program enrollment covered at least one day of that month.
Apps ScriptGoogle's built-in scripting tool for Sheets, Drive, and Gmail. Our automations in the automation/ folder run there.
As-of dateThe date a rule is measured on. For monthly reporting it is usually the last day of the reporting month.
BAABusiness Associate Agreement. The contract that lets a vendor (for example Google) store or process PHI for Vayu Health.
BaselineA member's first relevant measurement for an enrollment, picked as the one closest to the enrollment start date. Used to measure change over time.
BGMBlood glucose meter: a finger-stick device a member uses to check blood sugar. Sprite may call it SMBGS.
BHBehavioral health: mental health services such as therapy or counseling.
BigQueryGoogle's cloud database where the pipeline stores its tables.
Billed touchAn ECM activity or encounter whose status is Billed, meaning it was submitted for payment.
Care teamThe Vayu program team that serves a member: Sequoia, Redwoods, Mariposa, or Sundial. The Outreach Team is not a program care team.
CDCESCertified Diabetes Care and Education Specialist.
CGMContinuous glucose monitor: a sensor worn on the body that measures blood sugar all day (for example Dexcom or Libre).
CINClient Index Number: the Medi-Cal member ID. It is PHI. Never type a real one into GitHub.
ColabGoogle Colaboratory: the browser notebooks in notebooks/ that run the monthly pipeline.
COREThe BigQuery layer where SQL turns raw source data into business meaning (for example enrollment episodes). CORE objects are views.
CozevaThe health plan quality platform that Stanislaus County member lists come from.
CrosswalkA table that links one system's member ID to another's. The Flourish crosswalk links Flourish IDs to CINs.
Cumulative MembersThe permanent list of every Vayu enrollment episode, kept in MART_Cumulative_Members.
DDSDiabetes Distress Scale: a short survey about how stressful living with diabetes feels.
DenominatorThe bottom number of a rate: the group being measured.
DiscontinuationWhen a member leaves the program. The reason comes from the MIF.
ECMEnhanced Care Management: the Medi-Cal (CalAIM) benefit that pays Vayu to coordinate care for members with complex needs.
ECM TrackerThe Sprite report that lists members in outreach and enrollment, with their status and dates.
ECM+Vayu's extra clinical services on top of ECM: billed visits with behavioral health (BH) clinicians or dietitian and diabetes educators (RDCDCES).
EHRElectronic health record. Vayu's EHR is Sprite.
EncounterOne documented visit or service between a staff member and a member.
Enrollment episodeOne stretch of program enrollment, from an enrollment start date to its end. A member can have more than one episode.
FlourishThe vendor platform where members complete the PAM survey.
GAD-7A 7-question anxiety screening survey.
GMIGlucose Management Indicator: an estimate of A1c calculated from CGM readings.
GraduationA discontinuation because the member met their care plan goals.
GrainWhat one row in a table stands for, for example one member per month.
HbA1cSame as A1c.
HIEHealth information exchange: a network that shares hospital and clinic records. MedEx is the monthly member list prepared for it.
Looker StudioGoogle's dashboard tool. The sourcing dashboards are built there.
MARTThe BigQuery layer of analysis-ready tables, such as one row per member or per member per month.
MCPManaged care plan: the Medi-Cal health plan a member belongs to (for example Health Net or Partnership HealthPlan).
Member-monthOne row for each member for each month they were in the program. Used to count members and services by month.
MERGEA database step that updates rows that already exist and adds rows that are new, without duplicating them.
MIFMember Information File: the ECM file shared with the health plan. It holds discontinuation reasons and population-of-focus flags.
NumeratorThe top number of a rate: the members who meet the condition being measured.
PAMPatient Activation Measure: a 13-question survey (scored 0 to 100, levels 1 to 4) about how ready a person is to manage their own health.
PathwayHow a member's diabetes is tracked: CGM, BGM, or A1c. Based on their devices and recent readings.
PHCPartnership HealthPlan of California, the health plan for Humboldt and Shasta counties.
PHIProtected health information: anything that identifies a person together with their health or care, such as a CIN, name, date of birth, or diagnosis.
PHQ-9A 9-question depression screening survey.
POFPopulation of focus: the ECM eligibility group a member qualifies under.
Pop BuilderPopulation Builder, the Sprite report tool used for the Enrolled Members and Discontinuations exports.
PRAPAREA standard survey about social needs (housing, food, transportation, and more).
PRODThe BigQuery layer of approved views. Tableau and Connected Sheets connect only to PROD.
RDCDCESA staff member who is both a Registered Dietitian and a Certified Diabetes Care and Education Specialist.
Reporting monthThe calendar month a report covers, written YYYY-MM (for example 2026-10).
SDOHSocial drivers (or determinants) of health: living conditions such as housing, food, and transportation that affect health.
Sourcing listA monthly list of possibly eligible members that a county or health plan sends to Vayu for outreach.
SpriteSprite Health, Vayu's EHR and care management system. Monthly reports are exported from it.
STGStaging: the BigQuery layer that holds source files as they arrived, with light cleanup only.
TableauThe dashboard tool Vayu uses for program reporting.
Verified eligibleA sourcing-list member whom the team confirmed meets the program rules and can be contacted.
ViewA saved database query that always shows current data. Unlike a table, it stores no rows of its own.
VW_Prefix for PROD views that dashboards read, for example VW_Member_Month.
