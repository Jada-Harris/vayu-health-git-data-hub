Data Dictionary / ECM and ECM+ services

% Members with 2+ / 3+ / 4+ ECM+ encounters

Metric % Members with 2+ / 3+ / 4+ ECM+ encounters

In plain terms

Among members with at least one ECM+ encounter in the month, the share who had 2 or more, 3 or more, or 4 or more.

Business definition

Denominator: members with at least 1 ECM+ encounter that month (# of Members with 1+ ECM+ Encounter). Not limited to active_during_month.

Format as Percentage.

Terms used on this page

  • Denominator: The bottom number of a rate: the group being measured.
  • ECM: Enhanced Care Management: the Medi-Cal (CalAIM) benefit that pays Vayu to coordinate care for members with complex needs.
  • ECM+: Vayu's extra clinical services on top of ECM: billed visits with behavioral health (BH) clinicians or dietitian and diabetes educators (RDCDCES).
  • Encounter: One documented visit or service between a staff member and a member.
