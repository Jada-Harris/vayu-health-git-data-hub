Data Dictionary / Appointments and visits

Visit flags

Metric Completed appointments · Visit flags

In plain terms

How each appointment is counted as a Phase 1 visit or another visit type, based on its service name.

Business definition

Match uses service_name after trimming, collapsing spaces, folding unicode dashes to -, and ignoring capitalization. (In-person) and (In-Person) are the same visit. If the parenthetical is stored in service_setting instead, service_name plus that setting is matched too. Lists are not mutually exclusive. A PCP in-person service sets both chw_in_person_visits and pcp_visits to 1. Recruiting at PCP Visit is Phase 1 only, not a PCP visit.

Phase 1 visits (phase_1_visits, Tableau # of Phase 1 Visits)

  • Recruiting at PCP Visit - 90 min (In-person)
  • Recruiting at PCP Visit -120 min (In-person)
  • Recruiting in Home or Community - 90 min (In-person)
  • Recruiting in Home or Community - 120 min (In-person)

CHW in-person visits (chw_in_person_visits, Tableau # of CHW In-Person Visits)

  • CHW Visit (Self-ID Goals)-60 min (In-person)
  • CHW Visit (ECM Assessment)-60 min (In-person)
  • CHW Visit-90 min (In-person home visit/community)
  • CHW Visit-120 min (In-person home visit/community)
  • CHW - PCP Visit - 90 min (In-Person)
  • CHW - PCP Visit - 120 min (In-Person)

CHW remote visits (chw_remote_visits, Tableau # of CHW Remote Visits)

  • CHW Visit (Self-ID Goals)-30 min (Telehealth)
  • CHW Visit (Self-ID Goals)-60 min (Telehealth)
  • CHW Visit (ECM Assessment)-60 min (Telehealth)
  • CHW Visit-30 min (Telehealth)
  • CHW Visit-60 min (Telehealth)
  • CHW - PCP Visit - 60 min (Telehealth)
  • CHW - PCP Visit - 90 min (Telehealth)
  • Celebration Visit - 60 min (Telehealth)
  • Graduation Visit - 60 min (Telehealth)

PCP visits (pcp_visits, Tableau # of PCP Visits)

  • CHW - PCP Visit - 90 min (In-Person)
  • CHW - PCP Visit - 120 min (In-Person)
  • CHW - PCP Visit - 60 min (Telehealth)
  • CHW - PCP Visit - 90 min (Telehealth)

If Sprite renames a service, add the new string to the appointment visit-flag rules and to this list. Otherwise the flag stays 0 and the service name still appears on the appointment row.

Terms used on this page

  • ECM: Enhanced Care Management: the Medi-Cal (CalAIM) benefit that pays Vayu to coordinate care for members with complex needs.
  • Graduation: A discontinuation because the member met their care plan goals.
  • Sprite: Sprite Health, Vayu's EHR and care management system. Monthly reports are exported from it.
  • Tableau: The dashboard tool Vayu uses for program reporting.
