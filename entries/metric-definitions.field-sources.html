Field sources

Metric Field sources

In plain terms

Where two member fields come from: population of focus (POF) from the Demographics tab, and discontinuation reasons from the MIF.

Business definition

On MERGE, a non-blank MIF reason or code replaces the stored value. A blank MIF value does not clear a reason already on MART, except when the Enrolled stale-discontinuation rule below clears discontinuation_date — then reason/code are cleared too.

Terms used on this page

  • MART: The BigQuery layer of analysis-ready tables, such as one row per member or per member per month.
  • MERGE: A database step that updates rows that already exist and adds rows that are new, without duplicating them.
  • MIF: Member Information File: the ECM file shared with the health plan. It holds discontinuation reasons and population-of-focus flags.
  • POF: Population of focus: the ECM eligibility group a member qualifies under.
