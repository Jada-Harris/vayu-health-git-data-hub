D. Subsequent enrollment

Rule D. Subsequent enrollment

In plain terms

Whether a member from a sourcing list later enrolled in the program, and when.

Business definition

Documented event: MIN(ecm_enrollment_start_date) from MART_Cumulative_Members, unioned with current enrolled STG rows so a brand-new episode is visible before MART apply.

Multiple episodes do not inflate distinct-member conversion. Enrollment without a verified date_moved_to_outreach is flagged as enrolled_without_verified_outreach and is excluded from enrolled conversion.

Terms used on this page

  • MART: The BigQuery layer of analysis-ready tables, such as one row per member or per member per month.
  • STG: Staging: the BigQuery layer that holds source files as they arrived, with light cleanup only.
