Data Dictionary / Social needs (PRAPARE)

PRAPARE / SDOH

Object PRAPARE / SDOH

Business definition

Python writes STG_PRAPARE_Assessments from the Sprite CSV. Tableau reads VW_PRAPARE_Assessments and VW_PRAPARE_Responses (current assessment version only). The EHR CSV is a full-history extract; reruns MERGE on source_row_hash and do not delete prior STG rows.

Shared lineage on PRAPARE STG: source_row_hash, prapare_assessment_key, source_file_row_number, source_load_date, source_extra_json, source_population = prapare. Loader reporting_month is the operational Drive-folder month. Created_Date stays on the row as exported.

Terms used on this page

  • EHR: Electronic health record. Vayu's EHR is Sprite.
  • MERGE: A database step that updates rows that already exist and adds rows that are new, without duplicating them.
  • PRAPARE: A standard survey about social needs (housing, food, transportation, and more).
  • SDOH: Social drivers (or determinants) of health: living conditions such as housing, food, and transportation that affect health.
  • Sprite: Sprite Health, Vayu's EHR and care management system. Monthly reports are exported from it.
  • STG: Staging: the BigQuery layer that holds source files as they arrived, with light cleanup only.
  • Tableau: The dashboard tool Vayu uses for program reporting.
