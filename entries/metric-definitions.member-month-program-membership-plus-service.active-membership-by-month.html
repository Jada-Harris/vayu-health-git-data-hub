Active membership by month

Metric Member-month (program membership + service) · Active membership by month

In plain terms

Whether a member counts as active, enrolled, or discontinued in a month, based on their enrollment start and end dates.

Business definition

Derived from MART_Cumulative_Members enrollment dates (not from STG month slices). Episode end = COALESCE(ecm_enrollment_end_date, discontinuation_date).

Member-month spine = event members for the month union members with active_during_month = 1. Tableau: VW_Member_Month exposes active flags as Yes/No.

Terms used on this page

  • Member-month: One row for each member for each month they were in the program. Used to count members and services by month.
  • STG: Staging: the BigQuery layer that holds source files as they arrived, with light cleanup only.
  • Tableau: The dashboard tool Vayu uses for program reporting.
