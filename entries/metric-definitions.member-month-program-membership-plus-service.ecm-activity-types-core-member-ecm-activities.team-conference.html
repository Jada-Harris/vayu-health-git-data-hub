Team Conference

Metric Member-month (program membership + service) · ECM activity types (CORE_Member_ECM_Activities)

In plain terms

A care team meeting about the member. It counts as a valid activity but is not billed.

Business definition

Flag / count: ecm_team_conference_flag / team_conference_count Activity types: Log Team Conference (also counted as a valid activity; filter in Tableau when service KPIs should exclude them)
