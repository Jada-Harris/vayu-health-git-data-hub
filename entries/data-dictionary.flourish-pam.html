Flourish PAM

Object Flourish PAM

Business definition

Flourish_Member_Crosswalk is one active verified mapping per Flourish identifier. It is not rebuilt by the monthly Sprite truncate/replace loads. Flourish_PAM_Assessments is the current full-history Flourish snapshot. Names, date of birth, postal code, and Q1–Q13 stay on that table.

CORE_Flourish_PAM_Assessments is one source row. Unmatched assessments keep a null CIN. VW_PAM_Assessments adds at most one MART_Cumulative_Members episode open on the completion date. reporting_month is the UTC completion month.

VW_PAM_Member_Month is one CIN, survey series, and completion month. VW_PAM_Member_Summary is one CIN and survey series. VW_PAM_Member_CIN_Filter is one row per CIN only when one series is configured. Definitions: flourish_pam.md.

Demographics on the PAM PROD views come from MART_Cumulative_Members whenever the mapped CIN appears on the cumulative book (verified crosswalk identity; Flourish name is not re-matched). Prefer the episode open on the Flourish completion date; otherwise the current/latest episode for that CIN. health_plan is the same as organization. partner and dm_type are separate cumulative-book fields (same meanings as on VW_Member_Month). Demographics stay null only for unmatched Flourish IDs, CINs absent from the cumulative book, or blank source fields on the picked episode.

VW_PAM_Enrollment_Outcomes and VW_PAM_MoM_Run_Chart are Flourish A1c-style enrollment/MoM products (all surveys pooled). distribution_pam_level / distribution_pam_score carry baseline when there is no post-180-day Flourish assessment. They are not the Trend paired views.

Terms used on this page

  • A1c: A blood test that shows a person's average blood sugar over about the last three months. Also written HbA1c. Higher numbers mean less controlled diabetes.
  • CIN: Client Index Number: the Medi-Cal member ID. It is PHI. Never type a real one into GitHub.
  • Flourish: The vendor platform where members complete the PAM survey.
  • PAM: Patient Activation Measure: a 13-question survey (scored 0 to 100, levels 1 to 4) about how ready a person is to manage their own health.
  • PROD: The BigQuery layer of approved views. Tableau and Connected Sheets connect only to PROD.
  • Sprite: Sprite Health, Vayu's EHR and care management system. Monthly reports are exported from it.
