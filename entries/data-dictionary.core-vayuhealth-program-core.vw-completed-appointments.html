Data Dictionary / Appointments and visits

VW_Completed_Appointments

Object Core (VayuHealth_Program_CORE) · VW_Completed_Appointments

Business definition

Tableau source. One row per CHW appointment with a Vayu Health or blank vendor, every status. Filter is_completed_appointment for completed-visit charts. reporting_month is the appointment month (DATE, first of month), not a single hard-coded month. Visit columns: phase_1_visits, chw_in_person_visits, chw_remote_visits, pcp_visits. appointment_status is the exported status, with completed normalized to Completed. region is grouped like the other Tableau views: Humboldt and Sierra (any capitalization) publish as Humboldt; Shasta, Tehama, and Trinity are each one title-case label so HUMBOLDT and Humboldt do not split filters. Appointment sheets do not carry region; demos come from cumulative book → ECM+ → Tracker. When that coalesced region is blank but county is present (common on Tracker outreach rows), region is imputed from county (strip a trailing County) and region_imputed_from_county is true. Prefer fixing Tracker/Sprite region upstream when possible.

Terms used on this page

  • ECM: Enhanced Care Management: the Medi-Cal (CalAIM) benefit that pays Vayu to coordinate care for members with complex needs.
  • ECM+: Vayu's extra clinical services on top of ECM: billed visits with behavioral health (BH) clinicians or dietitian and diabetes educators (RDCDCES).
  • Sprite: Sprite Health, Vayu's EHR and care management system. Monthly reports are exported from it.
  • Tableau: The dashboard tool Vayu uses for program reporting.
