Business definition

MART_Monthly_Members_Serviced / VW_Member_Month is the program member-month book, not a serviced-only extract.

  • Grain: one row per member_cin + reporting_month.
  • Who is included: every member whose enrollment episode overlapped the reporting month on MART_Cumulative_Members (still enrolled or discontinued during the month), plus any member with in-window ECM/ECM+ events that month.
  • With or without service: ecm_category = No service means the member had no ECM and no ECM+ service record in that reporting month — a missed opportunity on the roster. That includes members who were still enrolled and members discontinued in the month. Zero touch counts on those rows are intentional.
  • Tableau: use active_during_month / active_at_month_end for headcount trends; use ecm_category / touch flags for service charts (and filter Team Conference on the events view when needed). discontinued_in_month flags anyone whose ecm_enrollment_end_date and/or discontinuation_date falls in the reporting month.

Counting grain and deduplication

one row per `member_cin` + `reporting_month`.
