Data Dictionary / Clinical outcomes

HbA1c / GMI 6-month and most-recent (legacy, labeled)

Metric HbA1c / GMI 6-month and most-recent (legacy, labeled)

In plain terms

Older 6-month and most-recent A1c/GMI rules, kept so results match earlier reports. Each takes the lower of the A1c and GMI values.

Business definition

Kept for migration parity on the lower-of-markers pick. Both are limited to the enrollment episode window.

6-month: earliest HbA1c after day 180 and earliest GMI after day 180 (and on/before episode end), then the lower value.

Most recent: latest HbA1c and latest GMI with measurement date in [enrollment_start, episode_end], then the lower value.

On MERGE into MART_Cumulative_Members, 6-month and most-recent metabolic fields (and assessment most-recent fields) overwrite only when Incoming has a non-blank value. Blank Incoming does not clear a prior MART score.

Terms used on this page

  • A1c: A blood test that shows a person's average blood sugar over about the last three months. Also written HbA1c. Higher numbers mean less controlled diabetes.
  • GMI: Glucose Management Indicator: an estimate of A1c calculated from CGM readings.
  • HbA1c: Same as A1c.
  • MART: The BigQuery layer of analysis-ready tables, such as one row per member or per member per month.
  • MERGE: A database step that updates rows that already exist and adds rows that are new, without duplicating them.
