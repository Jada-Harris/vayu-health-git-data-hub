003. Window Median

Metric Calculations added from the snapshot · 003. Window Median

In plain terms

The median starting A1c (or GMI) across the members shown in the chart.

Business definition

Tableau name: Window Median.

Snapshot position 9 of 600 on this page. Workbook association: (Strategic View) Vayu Health Cumulative Members & Outcomes DB.

Approval status: Draft written from the Tableau formula on 2026-11-20, pending review by the data lead.

Check before approving: This is a table calculation, so the result depends on how the chart is laid out and filtered (those settings are not in the snapshot).

The same formula text also appears on this page at Window Median (Vayu Health Cumulative Members & Outcomes DB; position 14). That is a text match, not proof of one shared calculation or of equal dashboard results.

Table calculation. Addressing and partitioning are not in the snapshot.

WINDOW_MEDIAN(MIN([HbA1c/GMI Baseline]))

Terms used on this page

  • A1c: A blood test that shows a person's average blood sugar over about the last three months. Also written HbA1c. Higher numbers mean less controlled diabetes.
  • Baseline: A member's first relevant measurement for an enrollment, picked as the one closest to the enrollment start date. Used to measure change over time.
  • Cumulative Members: The permanent list of every Vayu enrollment episode, kept in MART_Cumulative_Members.
  • GMI: Glucose Management Indicator: an estimate of A1c calculated from CGM readings.
  • HbA1c: Same as A1c.
  • Tableau: The dashboard tool Vayu uses for program reporting.
  • View: A saved database query that always shows current data. Unlike a table, it stores no rows of its own.
