Data Dictionary / Clinical outcomes

Pathway

Metric Pathway

In plain terms

Each enrolled member is placed on one diabetes tracking path (CGM, BGM, or A1c) based on their active devices and readings from the last 60 days. When the device and the readings do not agree, the member is flagged as a possible device malfunction.

Business definition

Window: 60 days through as-of date, inclusive. Pathway is populated only when workflow_status is enrolled.

A device is CGM if device_type is CGM (or continuous-glucose) or device_name is one of the known Dexcom / Libre / Guardian kits.

A device is BGM if type or name is Self-monitoring blood glucose system (SMBGS) / SMBGS.

Active means Device tab status = Active (case-insensitive). No active device means there is no Active row of that type. That includes an inactive device and no device of that type.

cgm_type = CGM device name. bgm_type = BGM device type (Device tab type column).

Final pathway priority. The first matching rule wins.

A member is CGM Active only when they have an active CGM device and a recent 1-month GMI value. A member is BGM Active only when they have an active SMBGS/BGM device and a recent glucose marker. A member is a possible malfunction when the device and measurement data do not agree: either the active device exists but the expected recent measurement is missing, or the recent measurement exists but the matching active device is missing. Everyone else who is enrolled falls back to A1c. CORE leaves not-enrolled (including Exclude) blank.

Persistence on discharge: monthly MERGE into MART_Cumulative_Members overwrites pathway only when Incoming has a non-blank value. When a member is discontinued, the last known Pathway mapping sticks on the book (it is not written blank). MoM history uses that stuck value (or prior-month carryforward) for the discharge month.

Glucose markers: Glucose, Mean Plasma Glucose, Blood Sugar Fasting Marker, Blood Sugar Before Meal.

Pathway on MART_Cumulative_Members / VW_Member_Month is refreshed each monthly run for enrolled episodes and retained on discharge. It is not a month-over-month history by itself — use VW_Member_Pathway_Month for MoM counts.

Terms used on this page

  • A1c: A blood test that shows a person's average blood sugar over about the last three months. Also written HbA1c. Higher numbers mean less controlled diabetes.
  • BGM: Blood glucose meter: a finger-stick device a member uses to check blood sugar. Sprite may call it SMBGS.
  • CGM: Continuous glucose monitor: a sensor worn on the body that measures blood sugar all day (for example Dexcom or Libre).
  • CORE: The BigQuery layer where SQL turns raw source data into business meaning (for example enrollment episodes). CORE objects are views.
  • GMI: Glucose Management Indicator: an estimate of A1c calculated from CGM readings.
  • MERGE: A database step that updates rows that already exist and adds rows that are new, without duplicating them.
  • Pathway: How a member's diabetes is tracked: CGM, BGM, or A1c. Based on their devices and recent readings.
