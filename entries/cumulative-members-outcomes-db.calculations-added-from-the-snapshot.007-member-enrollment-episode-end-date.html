007. Member Enrollment Episode End Date

Metric Calculations added from the snapshot · 007. Member Enrollment Episode End Date

In plain terms

The date an enrollment ended: the earlier of the ECM enrollment end date and the discontinuation date, or whichever one exists.

Business definition

Tableau name: Member Enrollment Episode End Date.

Snapshot position 20 of 600 on this page. Workbook association: Vayu Health Cumulative Members & Outcomes DB.

Approval status: Draft written from the Tableau formula on 2026-11-20, pending review by the data lead.

Check before approving: The SQL pipeline uses the ECM enrollment end date first and uses the discontinuation date only when the end date is blank; it does not take the earlier date. When both dates exist and differ, Tableau and the SQL views give different end dates. Confirm which rule is correct.
