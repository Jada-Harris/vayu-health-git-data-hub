excluded_a1c_outside_12_months

Rule H. Sourcing-list file dashboard (canonical) · First exclusion reason

In plain terms

The A1c test is more than 12 months old (Stanislaus and Humboldt only).

Business definition

Stanislaus and Humboldt only, when the test date is before receipt_month - 1 year. Humboldt keeps a null test date. Stanislaus does not assign this reason when the Cozeva Demographics row has age at least 18, last_admission_date on or after receipt_month - 6 months, and the paired HbA1c row has a numeric result. NULL for Shasta (that loader does not apply this window). Order: 4

Terms used on this page

  • A1c: A blood test that shows a person's average blood sugar over about the last three months. Also written HbA1c. Higher numbers mean less controlled diabetes.
  • Cozeva: The health plan quality platform that Stanislaus County member lists come from.
  • HbA1c: Same as A1c.
