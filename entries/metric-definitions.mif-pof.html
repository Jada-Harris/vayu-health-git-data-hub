Business definition

Per CIN (joined onto each enrollment row):

  • mif_baseline_date = min created_date
  • mif_followup_date = max created_date
  • PCP / risk = latest MIF row
  • MIF adult population-of-focus flags are Yes unless the source is null, blank, or no
  • Those flags do not fill the published pof column. pof comes from Demographics.
