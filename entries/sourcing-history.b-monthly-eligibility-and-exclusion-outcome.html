B. Monthly eligibility and exclusion outcome

Rule B. Monthly eligibility and exclusion outcome

In plain terms

For each member on a list, whether they met the age (18+) and clinical rules, or were excluded because they were already enrolled, in outreach, declined, or outside the service area.

Business definition

MART_Sourcing_Outcome stores, for each received member + county + month:

  • whether they met a coded clinical path and age 18+ (met_clinical_eligibility)
  • enrolled / in-outreach / declined / excluded-city flags
  • concatenated exclusion_reasons
  • whether they remained after Program STG exclusions
  • whether they remained on the final outreach-ready list (also passes city filter)
  • evaluation date and rule_version

Later Tracker or Enrolled status changes do not rewrite a prior month. Only a rerun of that sourcing month replaces the slice.

Terms used on this page

  • STG: Staging: the BigQuery layer that holds source files as they arrived, with light cleanup only.
