Data Dictionary / ECM and ECM+ services

% Members with 1 ECM and 1 ECM+ touches

Metric % Members with 1 ECM and 1 ECM+ touches

In plain terms

Share of members enrolled during the month who had both a billed ECM touch and an ECM+ encounter.

Business definition

Numerator: ≥1 ECM billed touch and ≥1 ECM+ encounter. Denominator: members enrolled during the month (Members Enrolled During Month).

Format as Percentage.

This denominator is enrollment. It differs from the ECM / ECM+ volume % denominators (members with ≥1 of that service type).

Numerator

≥1 ECM billed touch and ≥1 ECM+ encounter.

Terms used on this page

  • Denominator: The bottom number of a rate: the group being measured.
  • ECM: Enhanced Care Management: the Medi-Cal (CalAIM) benefit that pays Vayu to coordinate care for members with complex needs.
  • ECM+: Vayu's extra clinical services on top of ECM: billed visits with behavioral health (BH) clinicians or dietitian and diabetes educators (RDCDCES).
  • Numerator: The top number of a rate: the members who meet the condition being measured.
