Data Dictionary / ECM and ECM+ services

% Members with 2+ / 3+ / 4+ ECM activities

Metric % Members with 2+ / 3+ / 4+ ECM activities

In plain terms

Among members with at least one ECM activity in the month, the share who had 2 or more, 3 or more, or 4 or more.

Business definition

Denominator: members with at least 1 ECM valid activity that month (# of Members with 1+ ECM Activity).

Format as Percentage.

Terms used on this page

  • Denominator: The bottom number of a rate: the group being measured.
  • ECM: Enhanced Care Management: the Medi-Cal (CalAIM) benefit that pays Vayu to coordinate care for members with complex needs.
