Vayu Health Sourcing and Enrollment Analysis

Historical and current sourcing activity through October 2026; outcomes reported through the documented observation cutoff.
Coverage Period: March 2025 – October 2026 | Outcome Cutoff: Nov 7, 2026 (Stanislaus List Ref: Oct 23, 2026)
Executive Summary
  • Overall Sourcing Yield: We received 3,463 members across all analyzed sourcing lists, converting to 724 verified eligible members, for an overall eligibility yield of 20.9%.
  • Goal Pacing: We have achieved 215 cumulative ever-enrolled members against a goal of 600. The net gap is 385 members with a target deadline of End of December 2027.
  • Shasta Target Precision: The Shasta Sep 26 sourcing cohort yielded 1 verified eligible member from 13 received (7.7%), with 5 remaining after initial criteria filters.
  • Pending Review Impact: A significant backlog exists in Humboldt. We currently have 687 members pending review from the Sep 26 and Oct 26 lists combined.

What this means: While historical yield (20.9%) demonstrates successful verification models, reaching our 600-member goal by December 2027 requires aggressively clearing the Humboldt backlog and optimizing conversion beyond the outreach handoff.

Important limitation: The 687 members pending review heavily understate recent eligibility yields. Attrition categories may overlap and should not be treated as a sequential mutually exclusive funnel.

Projection status: Goal pace tracking is available based on the 385 gap. Volume-based projections are excluded until staffing capacity and cost assumptions are supplied.

Research Question Results
B. Where are members dropping off between initial receipt, insurance/eligibility review, remaining after criteria, verified eligible, outreach handoff, and enrollment?
Fully Supported
Finding: Drop-offs are quantifiable from list receipt down to verified eligible and outreach handoff. A significant barrier currently is incomplete reviews (687 members in Humboldt). Valid yields show Stanislaus with the strongest conversion to eligibility (49.2%).
Important Limitation: Documented drop-off categories (like Missing A1c) may overlap and do not form a strict, sequential funnel. Enrollment cannot be list-attributed accurately at this time.
F. Analyze average list-processing time by Sourcing List Month/Year and County.
Fully Supported
Finding: List processing times vary significantly. Shasta typically ranges from 10 to 29 days depending on the cohort month, while Stanislaus completed its recent lists rapidly (9 to 25 days). Humboldt's latest lists remain pending.
Important Limitation: Missing/invalid member-level outreach handoff dates exist. The reported averages here correctly measure list-level processing duration (List Date Completed - List Date Received) and exclude incomplete/negative records.
A. Can current sourcing support the goal of 600 cumulative unique enrolled members?
Partially Supported
Finding: With a baseline of 215 members, we have a net gap of 385 members to reach the 600 goal by the end of December 2027. This demands ~28 net new enrolled members per month.
Recommended next action: Calculate required sourcing volume based on historic overall yield (20.9%), but integrate staffing capacity limits and true outreach conversion rates to validate feasibility.
E. Compare counties and health plans based on volume and yield.
Partially Supported
Finding: Stanislaus (Health Net) provides the highest historical yield (49.2%), but Humboldt represents the largest absolute volume of received candidates.
Recommended next action: Expand comparisons carefully, noting that Humboldt's recent data remains incomplete, lowering its perceived average yield overall.
D. How do members already enrolled with another ECM provider compare with members already enrolled with Vayu?
Recommended Follow-Up
Finding: We can identify historical volumes of members flagged as already enrolled in another ECM (non-Vayu).
Recommended next action: Target members who are already enrolled with another ECM provider to determine whether the outreach team can successfully convert them to Vayu. Track outreach attempts, successful contacts, conversion rates, reasons for non-conversion, and time to conversion separately from members already enrolled with Vayu.

Methods and Definitions

  • Data Sources: Historical Sourcing Analysis Workbook, BigQuery PROD Sourcing Dashboards, Cozeva sourcing demographic and clinical files.
  • Coverage & Cutoff: Sourcing coverage spans Mar 25 through Oct 26. Outcomes are observed and locked as of November 7, 2026.
  • Enrollment Goal: 600 cumulative unique members ever enrolled. Do not subtract discontinued members. (Current baseline = 215).
  • A1c Eligibility Rule: A1c ≥ 9% within the last 12 months; or A1c between 8% and 9% with an ED visit or hospitalization within the last 6 months.
  • Reporting Period: Metrics map to Sourcing List Month/Year (e.g. Sep 26), irrespective of when the review finished.
  • Exclusions: 'Lost Medi-Cal' and 'DSNP' populations are excluded entirely from this pipeline analysis.

Important Data Notes

Three sourcing lists were omitted from the analysis because they were unfinished files: Humboldt March 2025, Humboldt August 2025, and Stanislaus January 2026.
