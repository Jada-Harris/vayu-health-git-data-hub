Data Dictionary / ECM and ECM+ services

Member-month (program membership + service)

Metric Member-month (program membership + service)

In plain terms

One row per member per month for everyone in the program that month, whether or not they received a service, with their ECM and ECM+ service counts.

Business definition

MART_Monthly_Members_Serviced is one row per member_cin per reporting_month. It is the program member-month book: every overlapping enrollment that month (with or without documented service), including members discontinued during the month. Demographics coalesce per field: MART_Cumulative_Members → CORE_ECMPlus_Member_Demos → CORE_ECM_Tracker_Members. pof and discontinuation_reason_code are the cumulative-book values only (Demographics and MIF). Blank pathway is not enrolled, including Exclude.

Terms used on this page

  • ECM: Enhanced Care Management: the Medi-Cal (CalAIM) benefit that pays Vayu to coordinate care for members with complex needs.
  • ECM+: Vayu's extra clinical services on top of ECM: billed visits with behavioral health (BH) clinicians or dietitian and diabetes educators (RDCDCES).
  • Member-month: One row for each member for each month they were in the program. Used to count members and services by month.
  • MIF: Member Information File: the ECM file shared with the health plan. It holds discontinuation reasons and population-of-focus flags.
