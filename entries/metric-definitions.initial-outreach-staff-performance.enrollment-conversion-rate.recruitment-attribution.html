Data Dictionary / Outreach and enrollment conversion

Recruitment attribution

Metric Initial outreach (Staff Performance) · Enrollment Conversion Rate

In plain terms

A member counts as enrolled through outreach when their enrollment started on or after their first outreach. Sprite has no field that records this directly.

Business definition

Inferred: ECM enrollment start on or after first Log Initial Outreach. There is no explicit Sprite "enrolled via recruitment" field. Direct Referral tags are not used.

Terms used on this page

  • ECM: Enhanced Care Management: the Medi-Cal (CalAIM) benefit that pays Vayu to coordinate care for members with complex needs.
  • Sprite: Sprite Health, Vayu's EHR and care management system. Monthly reports are exported from it.
