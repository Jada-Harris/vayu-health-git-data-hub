Flourish PAM

Metric Flourish PAM

In plain terms

PAM scores and levels from Flourish, with each member's first (initial) and later (follow-up) surveys so change over time can be measured.

Business definition

Sprite measurement PAM on CORE_Member_Measurements and the cumulative book is a separate record. It is not an input to VW_PAM_Assessments. The full procedure is in flourish_pam.md.

reporting_month is the UTC date of the Flourish completion timestamp, truncated to the month. A missing or unparseable completion date has no reporting month.

An assessment is initial only when it is sequence 1 in a mapped CIN and survey series. A later sequenced assessment is a follow-up. Tied timestamps are not ordered, and a tied earliest timestamp leaves that series without a baseline.

PAM13 and PAM13_S are separate series. Language variants are separate until CORE_PAM_Survey_Series_Map says otherwise. Delivery mode does not start a new series.

Average paired change uses one follow-up per member and series. Monthly charts use the selected follow-up in VW_PAM_Member_Month. Overall charts use the latest follow-up in VW_PAM_Member_Summary. Do not average the monthly averages, and do not treat an initial row as zero improvement.

meets_enrollment_tenure_180_days is optional enrollment tenure at the Flourish completion date. It is not a six-month follow-up, and these fields are not a reproduction of Flourish PAM Trend.

Level member counts on a monthly bar are COUNT(DISTINCT member_cin) on the selected monthly row. Summing is_pam_level_1 counts assessments. A member with two assessments in one month can appear in two levels on the assessment view. VW_PAM_Member_Month is the mutually exclusive monthly level.

No PAM available is a missing CIN-filter match or a summary row with no sequenced level. It is not PAM level 1.

Terms used on this page

  • CIN: Client Index Number: the Medi-Cal member ID. It is PHI. Never type a real one into GitHub.
  • Flourish: The vendor platform where members complete the PAM survey.
  • PAM: Patient Activation Measure: a 13-question survey (scored 0 to 100, levels 1 to 4) about how ready a person is to manage their own health.
  • Sprite: Sprite Health, Vayu's EHR and care management system. Monthly reports are exported from it.
