Business definition

MART_Cumulative_Members is the persistent member book (“Cumulative” lives here). Historical seed through 2026-08-31 is complete (MIG_Tableau_Cumulative_Members / cleaned base). Do not reseed or delete historical rows. Monthly applies MERGE this month's Incoming into that table: update matching keys, insert new enrollments, never delete rows that are only in the base. The book stores pipeline snake_case names. VW_Cumulative_Members publishes the seeded cumulative-file headers (workflowStatus, Pathway, Phase_1_Days, HbA1c_GMI_Baseline, and the rest of that file) so Tableau can keep those fields. It also publishes pathway and discontinuation_reason_code under those exact names. Columns that are not on the file, including clinical markers and device flags used by the phase and pathway calculations, are still on the view under their stored names. region on that view is grouped: Humboldt and Sierra become Humboldt; Shasta, Tehama, and Trinity are each one title-case label.

On MATCH, baselines, enrollment end date, discontinuation date, Phase 1–4 dates/days, and care_coordinator_team are fill-blank only. discontinuation_reason and discontinuation_reason_code come from the MIF tab and replace the stored value only when MIF has a value. Six-month / most-recent A1c and assessment most-recent overwrite only when Incoming has a value. Pathway, workflow status, and other non-locked fields still refresh from CORE. pof is the Demographics column. dm_type / direct_referral are computed on Incoming from tags. A confirmed enrollment requires a start date and care team Sequoia, Redwoods, Mariposa, or Sundial. Rows already on MART that fail that test are not deleted; they drop out of the monthly update until the stored team is an official team.

MART_Monthly_Members_Serviced is one row per member_cin + reporting_month. Purpose: the program member-month book — every member whose enrollment episode overlapped the reporting month (still enrolled or discontinued during the month), whether or not they had documented ECM/ECM+ service that month. Spine = ECM/ECM+ event members for the month union members with an overlapping enrollment episode on MART_Cumulative_Members (active_during_month = 1). Service fields come from MART_Monthly_ECM_ECMPlus_Events; ecm_category = No service means no ECM and no ECM+ that month (missed opportunity on the roster), including members discontinued in-month. Coalesce order (first non-null wins per field): (1) MART_Cumulative_Members with flexible name matching — episode preference exact ecm_enrollment_start_date → overlapping episode in the reporting month → latest cumulative row for that CIN; (2) CORE_ECMPlus_Member_Demos; (3) CORE_ECM_Tracker_Members. pathway comes only from that cumulative episode (ECM+ and Tracker pathway values are not used). pof and discontinuation_reason_code come from the cumulative book only. Blank pathway is not enrolled, including Exclude. Key service fields: ecm_category (ECM only / ECM+ only / ECM and ECM+ / No service), ecm_valid_activity_count, ecm_billed_touch_count, bh_count, rd_count, bh_or_rd_count, both_bh_and_rd_flag, no_service_in_the_month. Active membership flags: active_during_month, active_at_month_end, new_enrollment_in_month, discontinued_in_month (discontinued_in_month = end date and/or discontinuation date falls in the reporting month; see docs/metric_definitions.md).
