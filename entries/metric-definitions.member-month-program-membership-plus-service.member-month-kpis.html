Data Dictionary / ECM and ECM+ services

Member-month KPIs

Metric Member-month (program membership + service) · Member-month KPIs

In plain terms

The counts on each member-month row (billed ECM touches and BH and RD encounters) and the Yes/No flags Tableau uses.

Business definition

Counts are event-row totals for the member + month (not unique calendar days):

  • Tableau Yes/No on VW_Member_Month: bh_encounter_flag (bh_count > 0), rd_encounter_flag (rd_count > 0), ecmplus_encounter_flag (bh_or_rd_count > 0), plus existing ecm_billed_touches_flag.
  • Avg. Member Touches (Tableau) — typically AVG(ecm_billed_touch_count + bh_or_rd_count) over members in the month

Workbook formulas (copy-paste Tableau calcs): docs/tableau/member_month_kpis.md.

Upstream guards against inflated counts: ECM+ loader drops Sprite claim-line columns (claim_*) that fan one encounter into many CSV rows, collapses the extract to one row per encounter_id, then skips encounter_ids already in STG (monthly files may include other months; composite fallback if id is blank); CORE_ECMPlus_Encounters and CORE_Member_Activity_Log also dedupe overlapping STG slices; CORE drops out-of-enrollment ECM/ECM+ rows and non-BH/RD ECM+ staff.

Terms used on this page

  • BH: Behavioral health: mental health services such as therapy or counseling.
  • CORE: The BigQuery layer where SQL turns raw source data into business meaning (for example enrollment episodes). CORE objects are views.
  • ECM: Enhanced Care Management: the Medi-Cal (CalAIM) benefit that pays Vayu to coordinate care for members with complex needs.
  • ECM+: Vayu's extra clinical services on top of ECM: billed visits with behavioral health (BH) clinicians or dietitian and diabetes educators (RDCDCES).
  • Member-month: One row for each member for each month they were in the program. Used to count members and services by month.
  • Sprite: Sprite Health, Vayu's EHR and care management system. Monthly reports are exported from it.
  • STG: Staging: the BigQuery layer that holds source files as they arrived, with light cleanup only.
  • Tableau: The dashboard tool Vayu uses for program reporting.
