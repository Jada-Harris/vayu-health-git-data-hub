Data Dictionary / Social needs (PRAPARE)

PRAPARE

Metric PRAPARE

In plain terms

Results of the PRAPARE social-needs survey: one row per current assessment, plus one row per answered question or unmet need.

Business definition

Tableau: VW_PRAPARE_Assessments (one row per current assessment) and VW_PRAPARE_Responses (one row per answered question, including split unmet needs). reporting_month on those views is YYYY-MM from Created_Date when Sprite leaves completion date blank. Tableau views keep classic SDOH plus is_homeless and are_you_currently_pregnant. Additive grouped reporting fields (Unmet_Need_Group, employment/housing/transportation groups and flags) are derived in CORE helpers and exposed on VW_PRAPARE_Assessments; raw survey columns are unchanged. Pregnancy/postpartum, EPDS, and infant-care fields stay in STG only.

Terms used on this page

  • CORE: The BigQuery layer where SQL turns raw source data into business meaning (for example enrollment episodes). CORE objects are views.
  • PRAPARE: A standard survey about social needs (housing, food, transportation, and more).
  • SDOH: Social drivers (or determinants) of health: living conditions such as housing, food, and transportation that affect health.
  • Sprite: Sprite Health, Vayu's EHR and care management system. Monthly reports are exported from it.
  • STG: Staging: the BigQuery layer that holds source files as they arrived, with light cleanup only.
  • Tableau: The dashboard tool Vayu uses for program reporting.
