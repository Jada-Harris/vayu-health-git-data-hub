Data Dictionary / ECM and ECM+ services

009. # of Members with 4+ Touches

Metric Calculations added from the snapshot · 009. # of Members with 4+ Touches

In plain terms

Number of unique members with 4 or more touches in the month. Here a touch is an ECM valid activity (follow-up, case note, team conference, or comprehensive assessment) or a billed ECM+ encounter with a BH or RD clinician.

Business definition

Tableau name: # of Members with 4+ Touches.
