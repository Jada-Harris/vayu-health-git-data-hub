Data Dictionary / Clinical outcomes

CGM Active (priority 1)

Rule Pathway

In plain terms

Has an active CGM and a recent GMI reading, so the CGM is working as expected.

Business definition

Active CGM + recent GMI

Terms used on this page

  • CGM: Continuous glucose monitor: a sensor worn on the body that measures blood sugar all day (for example Dexcom or Libre).
  • GMI: Glucose Management Indicator: an estimate of A1c calculated from CGM readings.
