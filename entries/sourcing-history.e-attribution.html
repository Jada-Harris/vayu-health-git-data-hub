E. Attribution

Rule E. Attribution

In plain terms

Which sourcing list gets credit when a member who appeared on more than one list enrolls (proposed rule, pending review).

Business definition

No prior attribution rule existed in this repo.

Proposed rule (implemented, pending review): credit the earliest sourcing_month + county where on_final_outreach_ready_list is true. One outreach event and one first enrollment attach to that cohort only.

Keep every received appearance. Source-grain funnel rows are cohort fate for members seen on that source (a member on two Stanislaus files can appear in both source rows). County, month, and all-months totals use attributed events so they do not double-count.

Distinguish:

  • Sourcing cohort reporting (VW_Sourcing_Funnel grain source/county/month): what eventually happened to members received in that month.
  • Calendar-month activity (VW_Sourcing_Activity_Month): how many attributed outreach additions and enrollments occurred in that calendar month.

reporting_cutoff_date is CURRENT_DATE(). Recent cohorts have had less time to convert.
