Data Dictionary / ECM and ECM+ services

ECM / ECM+ event eligibility (CORE)

Metric Member-month (program membership + service) · ECM / ECM+ event eligibility (CORE)

In plain terms

Which activities and encounters count as services: they must fall inside the member's enrollment and meet the staff and activity-type rules.

Business definition

Before flags and counts:

Phase metrics still use the full Activity Log, including Team Conference descriptions.

Terms used on this page

  • CORE: The BigQuery layer where SQL turns raw source data into business meaning (for example enrollment episodes). CORE objects are views.
  • ECM: Enhanced Care Management: the Medi-Cal (CalAIM) benefit that pays Vayu to coordinate care for members with complex needs.
  • ECM+: Vayu's extra clinical services on top of ECM: billed visits with behavioral health (BH) clinicians or dietitian and diabetes educators (RDCDCES).
