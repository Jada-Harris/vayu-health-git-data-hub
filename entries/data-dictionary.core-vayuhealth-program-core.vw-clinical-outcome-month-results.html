Data Dictionary / Clinical outcomes

VW_Clinical_Outcome_Month_Results

Object Core (VayuHealth_Program_CORE) · VW_Clinical_Outcome_Month_Results

Business definition

Tableau MoM freeze view. One published row per reporting_month × outcome_family (A1C, PHQ9, DDS, GAD7, PAM) with stored avg / median / STD, improvement rates, A1c control bands, and PAM level / score-increase metrics from the cumulative book at freeze time (eligible = VW_Member_Month active_during_month = Yes). Prior months are not recomputed. Member-grain audit: MART_Clinical_Outcome_Month_Member (PHI — not for Tableau). Apply after ECM: python/apply_clinical_outcome_month_freeze_sql_models.py --reporting-month YYYY-MM. Spec: docs/clinical_outcomes_reporting_month_snapshot_spec.md.

Terms used on this page

  • A1c: A blood test that shows a person's average blood sugar over about the last three months. Also written HbA1c. Higher numbers mean less controlled diabetes.
  • DDS: Diabetes Distress Scale: a short survey about how stressful living with diabetes feels.
  • ECM: Enhanced Care Management: the Medi-Cal (CalAIM) benefit that pays Vayu to coordinate care for members with complex needs.
  • PAM: Patient Activation Measure: a 13-question survey (scored 0 to 100, levels 1 to 4) about how ready a person is to manage their own health.
  • PHI: Protected health information: anything that identifies a person together with their health or care, such as a CIN, name, date of birth, or diagnosis.
  • Tableau: The dashboard tool Vayu uses for program reporting.
