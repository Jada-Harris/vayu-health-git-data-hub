Data Dictionary / Outreach and enrollment conversion

Monthly outreach conversion

Metric Initial outreach (Staff Performance) · Monthly outreach conversion

In plain terms

A month-by-month view of members outreached, added to outreach, and enrolled, by channel. It feeds the overall monthly outreach conversion rate.

Business definition

Tableau source: VayuHealth_Program_PROD.VW_Phase1_Monthly_Outreach_Conversion.

Grain: one row per member_cin + reporting_month + outreach_channel. reporting_month is a Log Initial Outreach month, an ECM enrollment-start month, or the Tracker month the member was added to outreach. A member outreached in August and enrolled in September has both months. A member with both channels in one outreach month has two rows. Use COUNTD(member_cin).

enrolled_in_reporting_month requires a Log Initial Outreach in the month and an ECM enrollment start in that same month. enrolled_in_month is any ECM enrollment start in the month. Outreach can be in an earlier month, including the end of the prior month, or absent. Do not use COUNTD(member_cin) as the denominator, because enrollment-only rows are in the view.

added_to_outreach_in_reporting_month is the Tracker member_created_date month. It includes members who were added and never outreached. Use reporting_month as the month axis so region, organization, health plan, care team, and channel filters match the conversion graphs.

On an outreach row, region, organization, health_plan, care_coordinator_team, and outreach_channel come from that month's outreach. On an enrollment-only row, those attributes come from the enrollment episode, and outreach_channel is the latest Log Initial Outreach on or before the enrollment start. On an added-only row, those attributes come from the member's outcome record, and outreach_channel is the member's earliest Log Initial Outreach. That channel is blank when the member was never outreached, so an outreach_channel filter leaves those members out.

Do not calculate the first-outreach cohort rate on this view. That rate stays on VW_Phase1_Enrollment_Conversion.

The monthly unique-outreached card on the events view counts members with any Log Initial Outreach in that activity month. Conversion uses first outreach month. Those two month counts will not always match.

Terms used on this page

  • ECM: Enhanced Care Management: the Medi-Cal (CalAIM) benefit that pays Vayu to coordinate care for members with complex needs.
  • Grain: What one row in a table stands for, for example one member per month.
  • Tableau: The dashboard tool Vayu uses for program reporting.
