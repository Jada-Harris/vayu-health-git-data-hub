Which Sprite activity types count toward each ECM measure, such as follow-ups, case notes, team conferences, and comprehensive assessments.

Business definition

Billing codes are assigned only to ECM Billed Touch activities.

Terms used on this page

  • Sprite: Sprite Health, Vayu's EHR and care management system. Monthly reports are exported from it.
