F. Funnel measures

Rule F. Funnel measures

In plain terms

The step-by-step counts from members received, to eligible, to added to outreach, to enrolled.

Business definition

Rates use SAFE_DIVIDE and a consistent cohort + cutoff:

  • remaining ÷ received
  • added to outreach to date ÷ remaining
  • enrolled to date ÷ added to outreach to date
  • enrolled to date ÷ received

Same heading

  • Added to outreach that month Metric
  • Added to outreach to date Metric
  • Enrolled that month Metric
  • Enrolled to date Metric
  • Members received Metric
  • Members remaining Metric
