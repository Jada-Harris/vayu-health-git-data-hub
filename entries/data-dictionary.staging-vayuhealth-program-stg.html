data-dictionary.staging-vayuhealth-program-stg

Business definition

Source-level tables for one reporting_month. Reruns delete and reload that month. Enrolled and discontinued workbooks both contribute all six subject tabs into shared STG_Member_* tables; source_population distinguishes origin. Rows are restricted to provider Vayu Health (New Life Discovery Project, Inc. and other providers are not loaded). Recurring STG names do not use “Cumulative”. Prior STG_Cumulative_Members_* and STG_Enrolled_Members_* / STG_Discontinued_Members_* are retired from monthly writes. MIG Tableau landing is one-time only (through 2026-08-31). PRAPARE uses a separate full-history MERGE into STG_PRAPARE_Assessments; it does not replace a reporting-month slice.

Shared lineage on every staging table: source_file_name, source_file_path, source_uri, source_population (enrolled / discontinued / historical_cumulative / activity_log / ecmplus / ecm_tracker / prapare), source_sheet, reporting_month, reporting_month_date, load_timestamp, loaded_at, pipeline_run_id.
