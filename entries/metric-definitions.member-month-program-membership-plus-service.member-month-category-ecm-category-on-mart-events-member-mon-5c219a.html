Data Dictionary / ECM and ECM+ services

Member-month category (ecm_category on MART events / member-month)

Metric Member-month (program membership + service) · Member-month category (ecm_category on MART events / member-month)

In plain terms

Labels each member-month by the kind of service received: ECM, ECM+, both, or no service.

Business definition

Also on the events MART: both_bh_and_rd_flag, no_service_in_the_month, bh_count, rd_count, bh_or_rd_count.

Terms used on this page

  • ECM: Enhanced Care Management: the Medi-Cal (CalAIM) benefit that pays Vayu to coordinate care for members with complex needs.
  • ECM+: Vayu's extra clinical services on top of ECM: billed visits with behavioral health (BH) clinicians or dietitian and diabetes educators (RDCDCES).
  • MART: The BigQuery layer of analysis-ready tables, such as one row per member or per member per month.
  • Member-month: One row for each member for each month they were in the program. Used to count members and services by month.
