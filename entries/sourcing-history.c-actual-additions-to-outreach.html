C. Actual additions to outreach

Rule C. Actual additions to outreach

In plain terms

Members actually moved onto the outreach list, based on the date the team entered on the county Outreach View.

Business definition

Documented outreach handoff: the latest Sheet row where Verified Eligible? is yes and date_moved_to_outreach is populated. On VW_Sourcing_Members that is effective_date_moved_to_outreach.

date_moved_to_outreach on the sync table is the raw Sheet value. Historical rows with Verified Eligible? = no used that column as the date the member was found ineligible. effective_date_verified_ineligible uses date_verified_ineligible when it is populated, and otherwise uses that old date only for a not-eligible token.

That date is entered on the county Eligibility Verification tab and synced by Apps Script. sync_timestamp, ingested_at, and current ECM Tracker status are not used as the month outreach began.

A member who remains on PROD_Sourcing is outreach-ready, not an outreach addition, until date_moved_to_outreach is present.

Terms used on this page

  • Apps Script: Google's built-in scripting tool for Sheets, Drive, and Gmail. Our automations in the automation/ folder run there.
  • ECM: Enhanced Care Management: the Medi-Cal (CalAIM) benefit that pays Vayu to coordinate care for members with complex needs.
  • ECM Tracker: The Sprite report that lists members in outreach and enrollment, with their status and dates.
  • View: A saved database query that always shows current data. Unlike a table, it stores no rows of its own.
