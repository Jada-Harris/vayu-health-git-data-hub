041. % of Members with Most Recent A1C >= 9

Metric Calculations added from the snapshot · 041. % of Members with Most Recent A1C >= 9

In plain terms

Share of enrollments with a baseline and a most recent A1c whose most recent A1c is 9 or higher.

Business definition

Tableau name: % of Members with Most Recent A1C >= 9.

Snapshot position 349 of 600 on this page. Workbook association: (Strategic View) Vayu Health Cumulative Members & Outcomes DB.

Approval status: Draft written from the Tableau formula on 2026-11-20, pending review by the data lead.

Check before approving: The top number does not require a baseline A1c, but the bottom number does, so members without a baseline can be counted on top only and the rate can be too high. Confirm the top number should also require a baseline.

Terms used on this page

  • A1c: A blood test that shows a person's average blood sugar over about the last three months. Also written HbA1c. Higher numbers mean less controlled diabetes.
  • Baseline: A member's first relevant measurement for an enrollment, picked as the one closest to the enrollment start date. Used to measure change over time.
  • Cumulative Members: The permanent list of every Vayu enrollment episode, kept in MART_Cumulative_Members.
  • Tableau: The dashboard tool Vayu uses for program reporting.
  • View: A saved database query that always shows current data. Unlike a table, it stores no rows of its own.
