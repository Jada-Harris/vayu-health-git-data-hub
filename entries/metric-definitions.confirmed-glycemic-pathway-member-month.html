Confirmed glycemic pathway (member-month)

Metric Confirmed glycemic pathway (member-month)

In plain terms

A month-by-month history of which pathway (A1c, BGM, or CGM) each member was on. It feeds the pathway trend chart.

Business definition

Grain: (member_cin, reporting_month) and ecm_enrollment_start_date when present.

Historical wide sheet (Google Sheet export): one row per member; month columns like 26-Feb / Jan 26 / YYYY-MM with cell values blank / A1C / BGM / CGM. The loader unpivots to long grain and loads only non-blank pathway months (pre-enrollment blanks, e.g. Jan–Jul for an August enrollee, stay in the sheet but are not written to MART). Identity columns: CIN, Member Name, Start Date, Care Team, LCM, CDCES.

Ongoing monthly (forward-looking only): when you apply cumulative members for a reporting_month, that apply also snapshots episodes that overlap the month — still enrolled or discharged in-month — from MART_Cumulative_Members into the pathway STG/MART/VW. Discharges keep their latest Pathway (MERGE does not blank it; snapshot also carries forward the prior pathway month if the book value is already blank). Re-running the same month replaces that pathway month slice. Do not reconstruct older months or re-export the Google Sheet for new months unless correcting history.

pathway_group is the MoM chart bucket: A1c / BGM / CGM / Other. Sheet A1C/BGM/CGM map to those groups. Cumulative labels such as CGM Active or Possible CGM Malfunction map to group CGM (detail stays on pathway).

Tableau: reporting_month_date on Columns, pathway_group on Color, COUNTD(member_cin) on Rows. Filter out blank/Other when the chart should show only confirmed A1c/BGM/CGM. Demographics on the view (region, organization/health_plan, care team, POF, etc.) come from the cumulative book join, so filters on this view match other member graphs.

Terms used on this page

  • A1c: A blood test that shows a person's average blood sugar over about the last three months. Also written HbA1c. Higher numbers mean less controlled diabetes.
  • BGM: Blood glucose meter: a finger-stick device a member uses to check blood sugar. Sprite may call it SMBGS.
  • CDCES: Certified Diabetes Care and Education Specialist.
  • CGM: Continuous glucose monitor: a sensor worn on the body that measures blood sugar all day (for example Dexcom or Libre).
  • CIN: Client Index Number: the Medi-Cal member ID. It is PHI. Never type a real one into GitHub.
  • Grain: What one row in a table stands for, for example one member per month.
  • MART: The BigQuery layer of analysis-ready tables, such as one row per member or per member per month.
  • MERGE: A database step that updates rows that already exist and adds rows that are new, without duplicating them.
  • Pathway: How a member's diabetes is tracked: CGM, BGM, or A1c. Based on their devices and recent readings.
  • POF: Population of focus: the ECM eligibility group a member qualifies under.
  • STG: Staging: the BigQuery layer that holds source files as they arrived, with light cleanup only.
  • Tableau: The dashboard tool Vayu uses for program reporting.
