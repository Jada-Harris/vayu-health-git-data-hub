Business definition

Tableau source. One row per CHW appointment with a Vayu Health or blank vendor, every status. Filter is_completed_appointment for completed-visit charts. reporting_month is the appointment month (DATE, first of month), not a single hard-coded month. Visit columns: phase_1_visits, chw_in_person_visits, chw_remote_visits, pcp_visits. appointment_status is the exported status, with completed normalized to Completed.
